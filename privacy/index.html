English

LifePocket is provided under the developer name mansy dev. This policy explains how the Android app and this policy website handle information. For privacy questions or requests, contact leorex5225@gmail.com.

Content stored on your device

Archives, information fields, records, tasks, dates, reminders, attachments and drafts are stored in the app's local storage. No account is required, and the app does not provide cloud synchronization. LifePocket does not intentionally upload your saved database, attachments or backups as application content to the developer or Firebase.

You choose files using Android's file or photo picker. When you save them, the app stores private copies without changing the original files. Android manages the selected file and directory permissions. Reminder delivery depends on notification and scheduling permissions and device background settings.

Trial, permanent unlock and Google Play payments

Google Play handles permanent-unlock payments and purchase restoration. LifePocket keeps the local trial start, access status, signed purchase proof and transaction confirmation status on your device to manage access. These records are separate from your saved content and are excluded from exported backups. The app uses Google Play to query purchases and confirm delivery, without a developer-operated payment backend. LifePocket does not receive your card details or add order tokens to its diagnostic logs. Google processes payment information under its own terms and privacy policy.

A trial starts on first opening and runs for 14 consecutive days. Backups, restores and local space management require permanent unlock, including during the trial. After the trial, app access requires purchase or restored purchase access. Your existing local data is retained while access is locked. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes app-local data and local purchase records; an eligible purchase can be restored through the same Google Play account, but this does not restore your saved content. Exported files are managed separately.

Backups and deletion

With permanent unlock, you can export saved content and attachments as a ZIP backup to a location you choose. Backups are not encrypted. If you select a third-party storage provider, that provider processes the exported file under its own terms and policy.

While you have app access, you can delete content within LifePocket. Local space management, including deleting another space, requires permanent unlock. After the trial expires, you must unlock or restore an eligible purchase before using in-app data management; local data is retained while locked. You can still clear app storage or uninstall through Android. Deleting an archive removes its direct information, attachments and archive-owned dates; independent records, tasks and custom dates remain with their archive link removed. Deleting an attachment does not delete its original source file. Uninstalling the app removes app-local content. Exported backups remain until you delete them from their storage location. A confirmed restore replaces the destination space's saved content rather than merging it.

Analytics and diagnostics

Starting with version 1.0.0 (4), usage analytics and crash diagnostics are separate, optional choices, both disabled until you explicitly enable them. Refusing does not affect app access. On first opening, or later under Profile → Privacy settings, you can save your choices or reject both. Privacy settings also remain available on the expired-trial screen. Choices are stored for this app installation, independently of local spaces and exported backups. Earlier internal test versions, including 1.0.0 (3), enabled collection by default and did not have these controls; update to the version with privacy settings to manage collection.

Turning an option off stops new reporting for that purpose. Analytics resets its local analytics data when disabled. Crashlytics may keep diagnostic reports locally even while uploading is disabled; the app discards reports from sessions that were not fully consented, and only authorizes eligible prior reports on a subsequent launch while crash diagnostics remains enabled. Withdrawal cannot recall transfers already started or erase previously received service records; those remain subject to retention and applicable deletion requests.

LifePocket sends fixed page names rather than your entered names, titles, dates, search queries or file paths, and does not set an account-based user ID. For the purposes you enable, the SDKs also process automatic events, session and interaction information, app-instance or installation identifiers, device and app metadata, crash stacks and diagnostics. Analytics automatic in-app purchase events may include the product identifier or name, price, currency and quantity; these are purchase-history data, separate from card details processed directly by Google Play. Analytics can derive location information such as country or city from IP-based processing; the app does not request GPS location permission. App configuration disables advertising ID collection and advertising personalization.

Manually reported errors omit the original exception messages and causes. Automatic crash reports follow the SDK's collection behavior and may contain exception messages or other runtime details. The filtering of manual reports does not cover every automatic diagnostic field.

Google processes this information to provide the services, potentially outside your country. SDK transmissions use encryption. This does not encrypt exported ZIP backups. Google signals is not enabled for the Analytics property. Account settings disable general Google products and services sharing, modeling and business insights contributions, and business recommendations; necessary technical support access remains enabled. Firebase sharing for non-Firebase service improvements and recommendations is disabled. Google may still process service-operation data to provide, protect and improve Firebase services under applicable terms. Service-operation data is distinct from your saved app content. Further information is available in Firebase privacy information and the Google privacy policy.

Intended audience

LifePocket is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you believe information from a child has been processed, contact us at the email above.

Retention and privacy requests

Local content remains until you delete it, replace its space through a restore, clear app storage or uninstall. You manage exported files separately.

Analytics is configured to retain event data for 2 months and user data for 14 months. New user activity resets the user-data retention period. These settings do not limit the retention of standard aggregated reports; eligible expired data is processed under Google's deletion schedule. See Analytics retention.

Firebase states that Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Other service data is handled under the applicable Google service policies.

For requests about information processed by the developer, use the contact email above. We may need additional information to identify relevant SDK records because the app does not associate telemetry with an account or email address. Local content can be managed directly in the app while you have access, or removed through Android by clearing app storage or uninstalling. Exported backups must be deleted separately. There is no account-deletion flow because there is no account registration. Applicable access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests can be sent to this address.

This website and policy updates

This policy site uses GitHub Pages and contains no analytics scripts, advertising, sign-in or third-party embedded content. GitHub processes website access information, including visitors' IP addresses for security, according to the GitHub privacy statement.

Updated policies will be published at this address with a revised effective date. Privacy and support contact: leorex5225@gmail.com.

简体中文

LifePocket 的开发者公开名称为 mansy dev。本政策说明 Android 应用与本政策网站如何处理信息。隐私问题与请求请联系 leorex5225@gmail.com。

保存在设备上的内容

档案、信息项、记录、待办、日子、提醒、附件与草稿保存在应用本地存储中。当前无需注册账号，不提供云同步。应用不会将已保存数据库、附件或备份作为业务内容主动上传给开发者或 Firebase。

你通过 Android 系统文件或相册选择器选择文件，应用保存私有副本，不修改源文件；文件和目录授权由 Android 管理。提醒送达取决于通知、调度权限和设备后台设置。

试用、永久解锁与 Google Play 支付

永久解锁付款与恢复购买由 Google Play 处理。LifePocket 在本机保存试用起点、使用权限、带签名的购买证明及交易确认状态，用于管理使用资格；这些记录独立于生活资料，不包含在导出的业务备份中。应用通过 Google Play 查询购买和确认交易，不设置开发者支付服务端。LifePocket 不接收银行卡资料，也不将订单令牌写入诊断日志；Google 按其条款及隐私政策处理付款信息。

试用从首次打开起连续计算 14 天；备份、恢复及本机空间管理在试用期间也需要永久解锁。到期后需购买或恢复购买权益才能继续访问应用，锁定期间本地已有资料保留。通过 Android 清除应用存储或卸载，会移除应用本地资料及本机购买记录；符合条件的购买可通过同一 Google Play 账号恢复，但恢复购买不会恢复生活资料。已导出文件需另行管理。

备份与删除

永久解锁后，你可以将已保存资料和附件导出为 ZIP 备份到自选位置。备份未加密；如果选择第三方存储服务，导出文件还受该服务条款与隐私政策约束。

在具有应用访问权限时，你可以在应用内删除内容；删除其他资料空间等本机空间管理需要永久解锁。试用到期后，需解锁或恢复有效购买，才能使用应用内资料管理，锁定期间资料保留。你仍可通过 Android 清除应用存储或卸载应用。删除档案会删除其直接信息、附件与所属模板日子，独立记录、待办及自定义日子保留，只解除关联。删除应用附件不删除源文件。卸载应用会删除应用本地内容，已导出备份需在相应位置另行删除。确认恢复备份后整体替换目标空间，不执行合并。

使用统计与诊断

从 1.0.0（4）起，使用统计和崩溃诊断分别自愿选择，明确同意前均默认关闭，拒绝不影响使用。首次打开，或后续在“我的 → 隐私设置”中，可保存选择或全部拒绝；体验到期页也保留隐私设置入口。选择独立保存在本次应用安装中，不随本机资料空间切换，也不进入导出备份。较早内部测试版本（包括 1.0.0（3））默认开启采集且没有这些控制，请更新至带隐私设置的版本后管理采集。

关闭选项会停止该用途的新上报；关闭使用统计时重置本地统计数据。自动上传关闭时，Crashlytics 仍可能在本地保存诊断报告；应用丢弃未全程同意的运行会话报告，仅在后续启动且崩溃诊断仍开启时授权发送合格的历史报告。撤回不能召回已开始的传输，也不会直接删除服务端已收到的记录，既有记录仍按保存期限及适用删除请求处理。

LifePocket 主动发送固定页面名称，不发送你输入的姓名、标题、日期、搜索词或文件路径，也不设置业务账号用户 ID。针对你开启的用途，SDK 还会处理自动事件、会话及交互、应用实例或安装标识、设备与应用信息、崩溃堆栈和诊断数据。Analytics 自动应用内购买事件可能包含商品标识或名称、价格、币种和数量，属于购买历史，与 Google Play 直接处理的银行卡资料不同。Analytics 可通过 IP 相关处理推导国家、城市等位置信息；应用不申请 GPS 定位权限。应用配置关闭广告 ID 采集及广告个性化。

手动报告异常会移除原始异常消息和原因链；自动崩溃报告遵循 SDK 行为，可能包含异常消息及其他运行信息，手动报告的过滤不覆盖全部自动诊断字段。

Google 为提供相关服务处理这些信息，处理地点可能在你所在国家或地区之外。SDK 使用加密传输，但导出的 ZIP 备份未加密。Analytics 媒体资源未启用 Google 信号；账号关闭通用“Google 产品和服务”、模型／业务洞见贡献及业务建议共享，仅保留必要技术支持访问。Firebase 的非 Firebase 服务改进与建议共享已关闭；Google 仍可按适用条款处理服务运行数据，以提供、保护及改进 Firebase 服务。服务运行数据不同于你保存的业务内容。详情见 Firebase 隐私说明与 Google 隐私政策。

保存期限与隐私请求

本地内容保存至你主动删除、从备份覆盖目标空间、清除应用存储或卸载；已导出文件由你另行管理。

Analytics 当前事件数据保留 2 个月，用户数据保留 14 个月；有新的用户活动时会重置用户数据保留期。这些期限不限制标准汇总报告的保存，符合到期条件的数据按 Google 的删除周期处理。详情见 Analytics 数据保留说明。

Firebase 说明 Crashlytics 将崩溃堆栈及相关标识保留 90 天后，开始从运行和备份系统移除。其他服务数据依适用 Google 服务政策处理。

关于开发者处理的信息，你可通过上述邮箱提出访问、更正、删除等适用隐私请求。由于应用未将遥测关联到账号或邮箱，可能需要进一步信息来定位 SDK 记录。本地内容可在具有访问权限时于应用内管理，也可通过 Android 清除应用存储或卸载来移除；已导出备份需另行删除。当前无账号注册，因此不设账号注销流程。
