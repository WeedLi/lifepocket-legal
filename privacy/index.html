LifePocket is provided under the developer name mansy dev. This policy explains how the Android app and this policy website handle information. For privacy questions or requests, contact leorex5225@gmail.com.

Content stored on your device

Archives, information fields, records, tasks, dates, reminders, attachments and drafts are stored in the app's local storage. No account is required, and the app does not provide cloud synchronization. LifePocket does not intentionally upload your saved database, attachments or backups as application content to the developer or Firebase.

You choose files using Android's file or photo picker. When you save them, the app stores private copies without changing the original files. Android manages the selected file and directory permissions. Reminder delivery depends on notification and scheduling permissions and device background settings.

Backups and deletion

You can export saved content and attachments as a ZIP backup to a location you choose. Backups are not encrypted. If you select a third-party storage provider, that provider processes the exported file under its own terms and policy.

You can delete content within LifePocket. Deleting an archive removes its direct information, attachments and archive-owned dates; independent records, tasks and custom dates remain with their archive link removed. Deleting an attachment does not delete its original source file. Uninstalling the app removes app-local content. Exported backups remain until you delete them from their storage location. A confirmed restore replaces the destination space's saved content rather than merging it.

Analytics and diagnostics

The release app enables Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics by default. There is currently no in-app switch to disable this collection. These services help us understand app usage and diagnose stability problems.

LifePocket sends fixed page names rather than your entered names, titles, dates, search queries or file paths, and does not set an account-based user ID. The SDKs also collect automatic events, session and interaction information, app-instance or installation identifiers, device and app metadata, crash stacks and diagnostics. Analytics can derive location information such as country or city from IP-based processing; the app does not request GPS location permission. App configuration disables advertising ID collection and advertising personalization.

Manually reported errors omit the original exception messages and causes. Automatic crash reports follow the SDK's collection behavior and may contain exception messages or other runtime details. The filtering of manual reports does not cover every automatic diagnostic field.

Google processes this information to provide the services, potentially outside your country. SDK transmissions use encryption. This does not encrypt exported ZIP backups. Google signals is not enabled for the Analytics property. Current account settings allow de-identified aggregate contributions for modeling and business insights, technical support access, and business recommendations; general Analytics sharing for Google products and services is off. Firebase service-data use for Google service improvements and recommendations is enabled. Firebase service data is distinct from your saved app content. Further information is available in Firebase privacy information and the Google privacy policy.

Retention and privacy requests

Local content remains until you delete it, replace its space through a restore, clear app storage or uninstall. You manage exported files separately.

Analytics is configured to retain event data for 2 months and user data for 14 months. New user activity resets the user-data retention period. These settings do not limit the retention of standard aggregated reports; eligible expired data is processed under Google's deletion schedule. See Analytics retention.

Firebase states that Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Other service data is handled under the applicable Google service policies.

For requests about information processed by the developer, use the contact email above. We may need additional information to identify relevant SDK records because the app does not associate telemetry with an account or email address. Local content can be managed directly in the app. There is no account-deletion flow because there is no account registration. Applicable access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests can be sent to this address.

This website and policy updates

This policy site uses GitHub Pages and contains no analytics scripts, advertising, sign-in or third-party embedded content. GitHub processes website access information, including visitors' IP addresses for security, according to the GitHub privacy statement.

Updated policies will be published at this address with a revised effective date. Privacy and support contact: leorex5225@gmail.com.

简体中文

LifePocket 的开发者公开名称为 mansy dev。本政策说明 Android 应用与本政策网站如何处理信息。隐私问题与请求请联系 leorex5225@gmail.com。

保存在设备上的内容

档案、信息项、记录、待办、日子、提醒、附件与草稿保存在应用本地存储中。当前无需注册账号，不提供云同步。应用不会将已保存数据库、附件或备份作为业务内容主动上传给开发者或 Firebase。

你通过 Android 系统文件或相册选择器选择文件，应用保存私有副本，不修改源文件；文件和目录授权由 Android 管理。提醒送达取决于通知、调度权限和设备后台设置。

备份与删除

你可以将已保存资料和附件导出为 ZIP 备份到自选位置。备份未加密；如果选择第三方存储服务，导出文件还受该服务条款与隐私政策约束。

你可以在应用内删除内容。删除档案会删除其直接信息、附件与所属模板日子，独立记录、待办及自定义日子保留，只解除关联。删除应用附件不删除源文件。卸载应用会删除应用本地内容，已导出备份需在相应位置另行删除。确认恢复备份后整体替换目标空间，不执行合并。

使用统计与诊断

正式版本默认开启 Google Firebase Analytics 和 Crashlytics，目前没有应用内关闭开关。相关服务用于了解应用使用情况和诊断稳定性问题。

LifePocket 主动发送固定页面名称，不发送你输入的姓名、标题、日期、搜索词或文件路径，也不设置业务账号用户 ID。SDK 还会收集自动事件、会话及交互、应用实例或安装标识、设备与应用信息、崩溃堆栈和诊断数据。Analytics 可通过 IP 相关处理推导国家、城市等位置信息；应用不申请 GPS 定位权限。应用配置关闭广告 ID 采集及广告个性化。

手动报告异常会移除原始异常消息和原因链；自动崩溃报告遵循 SDK 行为，可能包含异常消息及其他运行信息，手动报告的过滤不覆盖全部自动诊断字段。

Google 为提供相关服务处理这些信息，处理地点可能在你所在国家或地区之外。SDK 使用加密传输，但导出的 ZIP 备份未加密。Analytics 媒体资源未启用 Google 信号；当前账号允许去标识化汇总数据用于模型和业务洞见、技术支持访问及业务建议，未开启通用的“Google 产品和服务”Analytics 数据共享。Firebase 服务数据用于 Google 服务改进与建议的选项已开启；服务数据不同于你保存的业务内容。详情见 Firebase 隐私说明与 Google 隐私政策。

保存期限与隐私请求

本地内容保存至你主动删除、从备份覆盖目标空间、清除应用存储或卸载；已导出文件由你另行管理。

Analytics 当前事件数据保留 2 个月，用户数据保留 14 个月；有新的用户活动时会重置用户数据保留期。这些期限不限制标准汇总报告的保存，符合到期条件的数据按 Google 的删除周期处理。详情见 Analytics 数据保留说明。

Firebase 说明 Crashlytics 将崩溃堆栈及相关标识保留 90 天后，开始从运行和备份系统移除。其他服务数据依适用 Google 服务政策处理。

关于开发者处理的信息，你可通过上述邮箱提出访问、更正、删除等适用隐私请求。由于应用未将遥测关联到账号或邮箱，可能需要进一步信息来定位 SDK 记录。本地内容可在应用内管理；当前无账号注册，因此不设账号注销流程。

本网站使用 GitHub Pages 托管，无统计脚本、广告、登录或第三方嵌入内容。GitHub 按其 隐私声明处理网页访问信息，包括出于安全目的处理访问者 IP 地址。

政策更新会在此地址发布并修改生效日期。隐私及支持联系方式：leorex5225@gmail.com。
